## System requirements

### Minimal (just to start Fluorite)
- Any x86_64 CPU @ 1.5 Ghz with SSE2 support
- 4 Gb DDR3 RAM
- Any GPU
- UEFI support

### Minimal (to enable glass effect)
- 2 cores, 2 Ghz (Intel Core 2 Duo, AMD Athlon II)
- 4 Gb DDR3 RAM
- NVIDIA GeForce 8000 series, ATI Radeon HD 2000 series, Intel HD Graphics 2000/2500 (Sandy Bridge)
- UEFI support

### Recommended
- 4 cores, 2.5+ Ghz (Intel Core i5 8th gen, AMD Ryzen 3)
- 8 Gb DDR4 RAM
- NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050, AMD Radeon Vega 8, Intel Iris Xe
- UEFI support
